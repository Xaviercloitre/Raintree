Inside our international kindergarten in Bangkok

Every child arrives curious. Our teachers start from what your child notices, asks and loves, and build learning around it, guided by the British EYFS framework and a Reggio Emilia inspired approach. Raintree is accredited by ISAT and ONESQA, so you can trust the standards behind every day.

Read our approach →

  • Creativity

    Each classroom has an atelier, a small studio of real materials, where your child can try, make, wonder and show what they think.

  • Collaboration

    Kindergarten 1 and 2 share mixed-age classes. Younger children learn from older friends, and older children grow by helping. Classes stay small: 12 to 15 in nursery, 15 to 18 in kindergarten.

  • Community

    Your child hears English, Thai and Mandarin every day from specialist teachers. And you are part of school life too, from your very first visit to the move to primary school.

  • Nature

    Green space in the heart of Bangkok: nearly 2,000 sqm of gardens at Sathorn, and courtyards and an organic farm at Sukhumvit, so learning carries on outdoors.

  • Spaces

    Buildings designed for early years, with ateliers, a library, a cooking studio and a tree house, so there is always somewhere new for your child to explore.

How admissions work

Three simple steps, and we will guide you through each one. Applications are open all year, with one form for all three campuses.

    Come and see an ordinary school day at Sathorn or Sukhumvit, or register your interest in Bang Na.

  2. Apply

    Fill in one simple application form and choose your campus.

  3. Start

    Agree a start date with your campus team, and get ready for the first day.

Questions parents ask us

What ages does Raintree take?

From age 1 to 6. Your child can start in nursery, move up through kindergarten and finish with Year 1 (age 5+), ready for primary school.

What curriculum does Raintree follow?

We follow the British EYFS framework, brought to life through a Reggio Emilia inspired approach. In practice, learning starts from your child's own interests, with ateliers in the classrooms.

What languages will my child hear?

English, Thai and Mandarin, every day. Specialist Thai and Chinese teachers work alongside the class teachers, so your child hears all three throughout the day.

Where can I find the fees?

You will find current fees and policies on our Fees and admissions page. Applications are open all year, and the admissions team is happy to talk them through with you on a tour.

Where are the campuses?

Sathorn is on Nanglinchi Road and Sukhumvit is on Soi 26 in Klongtoey. Bang Na, on Bang Na–Trat Road (KM8), opens in August 2027.
