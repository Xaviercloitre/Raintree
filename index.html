Inside our international kindergarten in Bangkok

Raintree follows the British EYFS framework, brought to life through a Reggio Emilia inspired approach. Teachers begin with each child's ideas and interests, and give them room to grow.

  • Creativity

    Ateliers in the classrooms give children space to explore materials, ask questions and express their ideas.

  • Collaboration

    Small classes, 12 to 15 in nursery and 15 to 18 in kindergarten, each with a class teacher and assistants, so children learn with and from each other.

  • Community

    Children hear English, Thai and Mandarin every day from specialist teachers, and families are part of school life from the first visit to the move to primary school.

Questions parents ask us

What ages does Raintree take?

Children join from age 1 and stay to age 6, moving from nursery through kindergarten to Year 1 (age 5+).

What curriculum does Raintree follow?

The British EYFS framework, brought to life through a Reggio Emilia inspired approach, with ateliers in the classrooms.

What languages will my child hear?

English, Thai and Mandarin, every day, with specialist Thai and Chinese teachers working alongside the class teachers.

Where can I find the fees?

The current fees and policies are available through Fees and admissions. Applications are open year-round, and the admissions team can walk you through them on a tour.

Where are the campuses?

Sathorn, on Nanglinchi Road; Sukhumvit, on Soi 26 in Klongtoey; and Bang Na, on Bang Na–Trat Road (KM8), opening August 2027.
